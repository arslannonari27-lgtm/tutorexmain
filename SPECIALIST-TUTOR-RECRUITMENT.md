# Specialist tutor recruitment additions — TutorEx Academy

The existing public **Tutor Registration Google Form was not edited** by this update. Use these exact additions in Google Forms before recruiting O Level, A Level or IELTS tutors. Applications must remain private.

## New Google Form fields

1. **Which curricula or test preparation areas can you teach?** — Checkboxes: Pakistan Board; Cambridge O Level; Cambridge International AS & A Level; UK GCSE; UK A Level; IELTS Academic; IELTS General Training; Other. Tutors can select multiple but should demonstrate each selected specialization.
2. **Please specify your strongest subjects for each curriculum selected.** — Paragraph. Ask for exact subjects and syllabus codes where known.
3. **What relevant exam-board or IELTS preparation teaching experience do you have?** — Paragraph; do not require or imply prior experience if tutor is genuinely new.
4. **For IELTS tutors: which skills can you teach and provide feedback on?** — Checkboxes: Listening, Reading, Writing Task 1, Writing Task 2, Speaking, Not applicable.
5. **Can you demonstrate one curriculum-specific lesson and discuss assessment criteria?** — Multiple choice: Yes / No.

## Suggested assessment

- **O Level:** Confirm subject/syllabus familiarity, a representative explanation of a past-paper topic, clarity and grade-appropriate teaching. Ask the applicant to explain a sample marking approach; never claim formal Cambridge affiliation.
- **A Level:** Include advanced subject questions and a 10–15 minute teaching demonstration with a sample exam-style problem and feedback.
- **IELTS:** Check familiarity with Academic vs General Training and assess listening/reading task guidance, writing feedback and an interactive speaking demo. Never guarantee a particular band score or claim an official IELTS accreditation without evidence.

## Status & transparency

Until the assessment and identity/credential checks are complete, use **Applicant / Screening Pending** — never **Verified O Level Tutor**, **Approved A Level Tutor** or **Certified IELTS Trainer**.

## Parent inquiry workflow

Create a dedicated Parent/Adult Learner Google Form with program selection, subject/IELTS skill, Academic or General test type, exam board/syllabus or target band, location/time zone, timings and budget. Keep guardian consent for minor students. The website's present form is a copy/email composer only; its changes do **not** update any existing Google Forms automatically.
